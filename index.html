📜 Terms & Conditions

1. Maqsad (Purpose of Platform)

Fortube Quiz ek educational aur entertainment platform hai jo aapki knowledge (ilm) ko barhane ke liye banaya gaya hai. Hamara maqsad sirf paise kamana nahi, balke aapki thinking ability aur general knowledge ko improve karna hai. Yahan quiz khelna, tasks complete karna, aur spin karna — sab aapki apni marzi hai. Koi pressure nahi hai. Agar aap ko lagta hai ke yeh platform aapke liye theek nahi, to aap kabhi bhi ruk sakte hain. Hum kisi ko majboor nahi karte.

2. Khelnay ki Marzi (Free Will)

Aap yahan participate kar ke earnings kar sakte hain, lekin yeh mukammal tor par aapki apni marzi hogi. Har user ko apni financial situation dekh kar deposit karna chahiye. Hum recommend karte hain ke aap sirf utna hi paisa deposit karein jo aap afford kar sakte hain. Kisi bhi nuksan ke liye platform zimmedar nahi hoga.

3. Time & Voice Rules

Quiz mein answer 20 seconds ke andar dena hota hai. Jitni jaldi aap answer denge, utna zyada win karenge. Rules yeh hain:

  • 20 seconds ya kam waqt mein sahi jawab: Aapki bet double ho jayegi (2x reward).
  • Waqt khatam (timeout): Aap apni bet amount haar jayenge.
  • Ghalat jawab: Aap apni bet amount haar jayenge.
  • 200 Rs. wale card: Sirf 8 seconds milte hain — yeh sirf expert players ke liye hai.

4. Withdrawal Rules (Paise Nikalne ke Rules)

  • Minimum withdrawal: 100 Coins (Rs. 100).
  • Question requirement: Aapko apni last deposit ke baad kam az kam 20 questions solve karne honge.
  • Profit requirement: Aapka balance total deposit se zyada hona chahiye. Matlab aap profit mein hone chahiye.
  • Deposit lock: Withdrawal ke baad aapko dobara deposit karna hoga taake next withdrawal unlock ho sake.
  • Processing time: Withdrawal requests 24-48 hours mein process hoti hain.
  • Verification: Kuch cases mein aapko apni identity verify karni pad sakti hai.

5. Deposit Rules (Paise Dalne ke Rules)

  • Minimum deposit: 100 Coins (Rs. 100).
  • Maximum deposit: 100,000 Coins (Rs. 100,000) per transaction.
  • Payment methods: Sirf JazzCash aur SadaPay accept kiye jate hain.
  • Transaction ID (TID): Deposit form mein TID dalna zaroori hai.
  • Screenshot: Payment ka screenshot upload karna zaroori hai.
  • Approval time: Deposit 2 hours ke andar approve hota hai (working hours mein).
  • Fake deposits: Fake TID ya screenshot dalne par account permanently ban ho sakta hai.

6. Referral & Bonus Rules

  • Referral bonus: Har successful referral par Rs. 10 bonus milta hai.
  • Condition: Referred user ko kam az kam 100 coins deposit karne honge.
  • Daily bonus: Roz 1-2 coins free milte hain (24 hours ke baad reset hota hai).
  • Daily spin: Roz 1 spin free milta hai, jisme 1-5 coins jeet sakte hain.
  • Abuse: Fake accounts bana kar bonus lene par account ban ho jayega.

7. Account Security

  • Apna password kisi ke saath share na karein.
  • Public Wi-Fi par login na karein.
  • Strong password use karein (6+ characters, numbers aur symbols).
  • Agar aapko shak ho ke aapka account hack hua hai, foran password change karein.
  • Ek user sirf ek account bana sakta hai. Multiple accounts banane par sab ban ho jayenge.

8. Prohibited Activities (Manaa Kaam)

  • Fake accounts banana ya multiple accounts use karna.
  • Bots ya automated scripts use karna.

  • Illegal activities ke liye platform use karna.

In mein se koi bhi kaam karne par account permanently ban ho jayega aur balance zabt ho jayega.

9. Liability (Zimmedari)

Fortube Quiz kisi bhi financial nuksan ke liye zimmedar nahi hoga. Aap apni marzi se deposit karte hain aur apni marzi se quiz khelte hain. Platform kisi bhi waqt bhi terms change kar sakta hai. Changes ke baad bhi platform use karna matlab aap naye terms accept karte hain.
