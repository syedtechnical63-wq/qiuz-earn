📜 Terms & Conditions

1. Khelnay ki Marzi (Free Will)

Yeh app tashreehat (entertainment) aur zehni abiyari (mental exercise) ke liye hai. Aap apni marzi se paisy deposit karte hain aur quiz khel kar unhe double karne ki koshish karte hain. Kisi bhi qism ka dabao (pressure) nahi hai.

2. Deposit Rules

  • Deposit kam az kam 100 coins aur zyada se zyada 100,000 coins tak ho sakta hai.
  • Paisy JazzCash ya SadaPay ke zariye bheje jayen aur screenshot ke sath Transaction ID (TID) submit ki jaye.
  • Deposit approve hone mein 2 se 24 ghante lag sakte hain.

3. Withdrawal Rules

  • Withdraw kam az kam 100 coins ka ho sakta hai.

4. Zimmedari

Aap apne account ki security ke khud zimmedar hain. Password kisi ke sath share na karein. Quiz Earn kisi bhi qism ke financial nuqsan ki zimmedari nahi leta.
